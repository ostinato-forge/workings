MOCKUP F: THE WORKINGS CABINET. A literal arcade machine in the studio cabinet language: chassis, marquee with screws and ONLINE LED, speaker grille, four-tone CRT bezel with scanlines and vignette, perspective control deck, coin door. The cartridge identity is the trace-green phosphor, and the deck carries this course's own hardware: TEMPERATURE and TOP-K as physical rotary knobs beside NAVIGATE, START, and STOP. On the screen: the operator-bench lesson exactly as designed, prediction framing, no wagering anywhere. Static mockup: nothing is wired.
